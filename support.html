02Frequently asked

Where is my data stored?

On your device first. If you're signed in to iCloud, EditDeck also mirrors it into your own private iCloud container so it turns up on your other devices. There is no EditDeck account and no EditDeck server, and I have no way of seeing your data at any point.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account, ever. The app works fully offline. iCloud sync is the only part that wants a connection, and when it isn't available EditDeck simply carries on with the copy on your device and catches up later.

How does syncing between devices work?

Through iCloud, under your own Apple Account. Your records, note images and receipts travel in EditDeck's private CloudKit database. Audio notes and imported files go through iCloud Drive instead, so they download when you open them rather than filling every device you own.

There's no switch inside EditDeck. Sync follows your device: turn off iCloud Drive for EditDeck in Settings > your name > iCloud and the app runs on a local-only store from then on.

How do I back up my data or move to a new device?

If you use iCloud, a new device signed into the same account picks everything up on its own. For a copy you hold yourself, use the backup feature to export a single .editdeck file containing your productions, notes, audio, receipts and settings. Save it wherever you like, then choose a full restore to import a clean copy, or a merge restore to fold it into existing data. Backups happen only when you ask for one.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

Deleting EditDeck removes the copy on that device. If you've been syncing, the copy in your iCloud account stays until you remove it yourself under Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage. Export a backup first if you want something you can keep outside iCloud.

Which tax rates does EditDeck use?

EditDeck is built for the Australian freelance context and the July to June financial year. It applies the current ATO cents-per-kilometre mileage rate and the fixed-rate work-from-home method, GST at one eleventh, and superannuation at 12%. Records you've already saved keep the rate that applied when you created them, so updating a rate never rewrites past entries.

Why does the app ask for camera, microphone, photo or notification access?

Only to power specific features. The camera photographs receipts, the microphone records voice memos for your notes, photo access adds images you pick to your notes and library, and notifications show local reminders for your events. Nothing captured this way is sent to me or to any third party.

How do I request a feature or report a problem?

Email madebymikesupport@icloud.com. Feature requests genuinely shape what gets built next.

Is my data private?

EditDeck collects nothing, contains no analytics or tracking, and sends nothing to any server of mine. The full detail, including exactly what iCloud carries, is in the privacy policy.
