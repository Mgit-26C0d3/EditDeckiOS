Get in touch

EditDeck is built and maintained by one developer, so your message reaches me directly. Whether it's a bug, a question or a feature you'd like to see, I read everything and aim to reply within a few days.

Reporting a bug? It helps to include your device (iPhone or iPad, and which model), your iOS version, the EditDeck version from the bottom of the More menu, and what you were doing when it happened. A screenshot or screen recording is even better.

Your data and iCloud

Where is my data stored?

On your device first. If you're signed in to iCloud, EditDeck also mirrors it into your own private iCloud container so it turns up on your other devices. There is no EditDeck account and no EditDeck server, and I have no way of seeing your data at any point.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account, ever. The app works fully offline. iCloud sync is the only part that wants a connection, and when it isn't available EditDeck carries on with the copy on your device and catches up later.

How does syncing between iPhone and iPad work?

Through iCloud, under your own Apple Account. Sign in to the same account on both and your library follows. Your records, note images and receipts travel in EditDeck's private CloudKit database. Audio notes and imported files go through iCloud Drive instead, so they download when you open them rather than filling every device you own.

There's no switch inside EditDeck. Sync follows your device: turn off iCloud Drive for EditDeck in Settings › your name › iCloud and the app runs on a local-only store from then on.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

Deleting EditDeck removes the copy on that device. If you've been syncing, the copy in your iCloud account stays until you remove it yourself under Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage. Export a backup first if you want something you can keep outside iCloud.

Backups and new devices

How do I back up my data?

Go to More › Backup & Restore and export a single .editdeck file holding your productions, notes, audio, receipts, files and settings. Save it wherever you like.

If iCloud Drive is on, EditDeck also makes a backup once a week into its own folder in your iCloud Drive and keeps the latest four. You can turn that off on the same screen.

How do I move to a new iPhone or iPad?

If you use iCloud, a new device signed in to the same Apple Account picks everything up on its own; give it a little while on the first launch. Otherwise, open a backup file on the new device and choose a restore: a full restore replaces what's there with a clean copy, and a merge folds the backup into what you already have. A first launch also offers Restore from a backup.

Subscription

How much is EditDeck, and is there a trial?

EditDeck is one subscription, monthly or yearly, with everything included. The first seven days are free. The App Store shows the price in your own currency before you confirm.

How do I cancel?

In Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad. If you cancel during the free trial you won't be charged. Apple handles all billing, so refunds are requested through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I've subscribed, but a new device asks me to subscribe again.

Make sure the device is signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account, then tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen. Offer codes can be redeemed from the same screen.

What happens to my data if my subscription ends?

Nothing is deleted. It stays on your device and in your iCloud, and the subscription screen still lets you back it all up to a file. Subscribe again and everything is exactly where you left it.

Tax and money

Which tax rates does EditDeck use?

EditDeck is built for Australian freelancers and the July to June financial year. It uses the ATO's cents-per-kilometre rate for mileage with the 5,000 km cap, the fixed-rate method for working from home (or actual costs, chosen per year), GST at one eleventh, and superannuation at 12%. Rates are updated in the app when the ATO changes them.

Records you've already saved keep the rate that applied when you created them, so updating a rate never rewrites past entries.

Is EditDeck tax advice?

No. It keeps your records tidy and adds them up, and the accountant pack hands your accountant everything in one go. Check your return with your accountant or the ATO.

I'm not in Australia. Will it work for me?

The schedule, productions, notes, recordings, files, music and contacts work anywhere. The tax totals follow Australian rules, so treat them as a guide only elsewhere.

Permissions

Why does EditDeck ask for access to things?

Only to power specific features, and only when you first use them:

  • Camera: photograph receipts, take photos and scan documents.
  • Microphone: record audio notes.
  • Speech recognition: write transcripts of your recordings, on your device.
  • Photos: add the pictures you pick to notes and files.
  • Contacts: import people you choose, and optionally save EditDeck contacts back to your phone.
  • Notifications: reminders for events, productions and backups, scheduled on your device.

Nothing captured this way is sent to me or to any third party. The privacy policy has the detail.

If something's not right

My data isn't showing up on my other device.

Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, that iCloud Drive is on for EditDeck under Settings › your name › iCloud, and that the device has a connection. The first sync to a new device can take a few minutes; recordings and imported files download when you open them.

Reminders aren't arriving.

Check that notifications are allowed for EditDeck in Settings › Notifications › EditDeck, then look at More › Notifications in the app, where each kind of reminder and the quiet hours can be set.

Transcription isn't available.

Transcripts are written on your device, so they need speech recognition allowed for EditDeck and on-device recognition for your language. If your device can't transcribe on its own, EditDeck won't send the audio elsewhere to do it.
