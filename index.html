Clip 02 · The workspace 6 tracks

One workspace · every job

Everything around the edit,in order.

The reference material, the money and the schedule that a freelance edit generates, held together and linked to the production each one belongs to.

V1
Productions

Set each job's rate, dates, work days and holiday shutdowns. EditDeck works out live earnings, days remaining and contract progress from there.

V2
Earnings & tax

Gross, net and tax to the day, with GST, superannuation and the ATO mileage and work-from-home rates worked out for the July to June year.

V3
Bookings

A whole year on one timeline: days booked, days free and overlaps at a glance, so you know when you can take the next job.

V4
Notes & audio

Rich-text notes, voice memos with waveforms, moodboards, and Avid Media Composer markers you can re-colour and export back to Avid. Each one linked to its job.

V5
Files

Documents, images, audio and receipts, filed by production. Pin what matters, and restore anything you delete.

V6
Mileage & WFH

Log trips at the current ATO cents-per-km rate with the 5,000 km cap tracked, and home-office hours at the fixed rate. Export both to CSV at tax time.
