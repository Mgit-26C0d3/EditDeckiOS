The money

July to June, GST and superannuation, and the ATO's own cents-per-kilometre and fixed-rate home-office figures. Worked out as you go, not reconstructed in a panic.

7 productions booked this financial year

  • Gross, net and tax to the day. Earnings accrue while the contract runs, so the figure on screen is the figure you have actually earned.
  • Rates frozen per record. Every entry keeps the rate that applied the day you logged it, so changing a rate never rewrites your history.
  • Shutdowns respected. The earned-to-date curve goes flat across a holiday break instead of quietly overstating the year.
  • Expenses, GST and receipts. Claimable GST and deductible totals per financial year, with a standing warning for anything still missing a receipt.
  • Mileage and working from home. Trips at the current cents-per-kilometre rate with the 5,000 km cap tracked, and home-office hours at the fixed rate.
  • A what-if calculator and clean CSV exports for weighing up an offer, and for handing your accountant something they can read.

Private by design

Your work stays on your phone.

There is no EditDeck account and no EditDeck server. Everything lives in an on-device store. Switch on iCloud and it syncs through your own Apple account and nowhere else. Leave it off and nothing goes anywhere. The only other copy that ever leaves the phone is a .editdeck backup file you create and control.
