Privacy Policy

What EditDeck stores, where it goes, and who can see it. The short answer is: you, and nobody else.

Last updated 20 August 2026

EditDeck has no accounts, no servers of its own, and no analytics. It collects nothing about you and transmits nothing to me. Your data lives on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud container under your Apple Account.

01The short version

EditDeck is a local-first app. Everything you create is written to a store on your device. There is nothing to sign in to, no EditDeck server anywhere, and no tracking of any kind.

If your device is signed in to iCloud, EditDeck also mirrors your data into its own private iCloud container so it appears on your other devices. That container belongs to your Apple Account. It is not readable by me, and Apple's own privacy terms govern it.

02What I collect

Nothing. EditDeck gathers no personal information, no usage data, no device identifiers and no crash telemetry. I run no servers and have no mechanism to receive, see or store anything you enter into the app.

03What is stored on your device

Everything you create in EditDeck is written to the app's own storage on your device:

  • Productions, schedules, bookings and calendar events
  • Financial records: rates, earnings, tax, expenses, receipts, mileage and work-from-home hours
  • Contacts you add
  • Notes, audio recordings, moodboards, imported files and Avid marker lists

None of it is sent to me or to any third party. The only place it travels is your own iCloud, described next.

04iCloud sync

EditDeck uses Apple's CloudKit and iCloud Drive to keep your data consistent across the devices signed in to your Apple Account. It writes only to EditDeck's private iCloud container, which means:

  • The data belongs to your Apple Account, not to me. I cannot read it, list it or recover it.
  • Nothing is written to a public or shared CloudKit database, so it is never visible to other users.
  • Apple handles the transfer and storage under Apple's Privacy Policy, and the space it uses counts towards your iCloud storage.

Two mechanisms are involved. Your records, note images and receipts are mirrored through CloudKit. Audio recordings and files you import are carried by iCloud Drive instead, so they download when you open them rather than occupying every device.

Turning it off. There is no sync switch inside EditDeck; it follows your device settings. Turn off iCloud Drive for EditDeck under Settings > your name > iCloud, or sign out of iCloud, and the app runs entirely on the local store. EditDeck also falls back to a local-only store on its own whenever iCloud is unavailable.

05Device permissions

EditDeck asks for a few system permissions so certain features can work. In every case the result is stored with your other data and is never sent to me or to a third party:

  • Camera, to photograph receipts.
  • Microphone, to record the voice memos you attach to notes.
  • Photos, to add images you choose to your notes and library, and to save images you create. EditDeck only ever sees the items you select.
  • Notifications, to show local reminders for your events and deadlines. These are scheduled on your device; no notification is sent from a server of mine.

06Backups

EditDeck can export a single .editdeck file holding your whole workspace, so you can keep a copy or move to a device that doesn't share your iCloud account. Backups are created only when you ask for one, and what happens to the file afterwards is entirely yours to decide: where you save it, and whether you share it at all. EditDeck never uploads a backup anywhere.

07Third-party code

EditDeck includes one open-source library, ZIPFoundation, which reads and writes the backup archive on your device. It is a file-handling component only: it collects no data and contacts no network.

There are no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDKs in the app, and no code in EditDeck makes a network request other than the iCloud sync described above.

08Controlling and deleting your data

You can edit or delete any record inside the app at any time. Deleting EditDeck from a device removes the copy stored on it.

If you have been syncing, a copy remains in your iCloud account until you remove it yourself under Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage. Because that data sits in your Apple Account rather than with me, you are the only person who can delete it, and there is nothing for you to request from me.

09Children's privacy

EditDeck collects no personal information from anyone, including children. No data is gathered, transmitted or shared regardless of who uses the app.

10Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date at the top. Because EditDeck still collects nothing, changes are expected to be rare.

11Contact

Questions about this policy, or about privacy in EditDeck generally, can go to madebymikesupport@icloud.com.
